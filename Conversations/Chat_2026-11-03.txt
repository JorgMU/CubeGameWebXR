User: How much overhead is there to having AR and VR modes? Is my worry that being with in MR mode with an opaque skybox really valid?

Copilot: **Overhead of having both modes**

The code cost is negligible. There is one extra button, one entry in `HOMES`, and a blend-mode check. Nothing AR-specific runs unless you press START AR.

One small global cost: the renderer is created with `alpha: true` for every mode, because that setting can't change after creation. The only effect is a little extra blending work, which I'd expect to be minor on Quest.

**Is your worry valid?**

Partly. I haven't measured any of this.

- **Passthrough probably keeps running.** In an AR session the headset's compositor draws the camera feed first and layers your frame over it. A black sphere only covers the feed with opaque pixels. Whether the OS skips the camera work when it's fully covered isn't something I can confirm, and the app can't tell it to.
- **The cost is mostly on the system side.** Camera processing and reprojection happen in the OS, not in your app's GPU frame time. Your own rendering cost is about the same in either session type. The likely downsides are some extra battery and heat, not frame drops.
- **How big it is.** I'd guess small, but I don't have a number. That's why the two-button split is the right safeguard. If you want zero passthrough cost, use ENTER VR, which has no passthrough layer at all.

**Measuring it**

You can compare the two on the headset with `hzdb` performance tracing or OVR Metrics Tool (CPU, GPU and battery drain). I can help run that if you want a real answer.

**What exists to build on:** the controller menu is already a canvas panel on the left controller, operated by the right controller's ray and trigger. A slider is the same hit-test, except the trigger is held and the ray's position along the bar sets the value.

- **Saving:** I'd store the values in `localStorage` so they survive reloads.

**Where to put it**
- **A submenu in the existing menu:** simplest, with the least new input code. Add an "Appearance" row that swaps the panel to the sliders, with a Back row.
- **Right-controller panel on B:** doable, but the right controller is also your pointer, so the sliders would need the left controller as the pointer.
