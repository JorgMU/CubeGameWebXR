Quest: thumbstick rolls one square. Hold one grip to drag the world, or both grips to also rotate and scale: twist hands to rotate (yaw), move hands apart/together to scale. Press B button (right hand) to open the color picker.
Desktop: arrow keys / WASD roll, Ctrl+Arrow move world, Q/E rotate world, +/-/wheel scale world, 0 reset world, N next, R restart, P toggle debug info, T tilt board up, Shift+T tilt down, H hide/show this panel (the buttons at the top right stay visible), C clear scores.
The clock starts on your first move. Each match is worth 180 points minus one point per elapsed second (minimum 0). The top 10 completed levels are kept across sessions (saved in your browser, like the colours), and shown in the table above and on the scores panel beside the board. C, or the Clear scores button, wipes them.
In MR after a win, pull a trigger to start the next level.
