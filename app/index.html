Quest: thumbstick rolls one square. Hold one grip to drag the world, or both grips to also rotate and scale: twist hands to rotate (yaw), move hands apart/together to scale. Press B button (right hand) to open the color picker.
Desktop: the buttons at the top right do everything; arrow keys roll the cube and the mouse wheel scales the world. Copy Settings copies the session defaults to the clipboard as ECMAScript, ready to paste into app/sessionConfig.js. Reset Colors clears the saved colours and restores the server defaults (no undo; scores are not touched).
The clock starts on your first move. Each match is worth 180 points minus one point per elapsed second (minimum 0). The top 10 completed levels are kept across sessions (saved in your browser, like the colours), and shown in the main panel (and on the scores panel beside the board in MR). Clear Scores wipes them.
In MR after a win, pull a trigger to start the next level.
